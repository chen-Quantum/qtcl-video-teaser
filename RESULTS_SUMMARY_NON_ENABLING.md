# Results Summary (high-level, non-enabling)

A **private research prototype** explores **quantum-inspired temporal control
for video**: given real or real-looking video, it aims to make motion and
on-screen change progress more smoothly over time while a chosen edit (such as a
colour or background change) is applied.

The demos in `media/` show short before/after clips on synthetic and
public-domain test imagery. They are visual illustrations only.

## Honest notes
- This is **early research**, not a product and not state-of-the-art.
- **No advantage of any kind is asserted.**
- On these tests, results are **competitive with, but do not clearly beat,
  strong conventional approaches** — a measured, honest result.
- The method, the technical details, and the settings are **kept private and are
  not included here.**

## Safety
Synthetic or public-domain test imagery only. No real private identities, no
public-figure identity edits, no minors, no face swapping, no deepfakes.
